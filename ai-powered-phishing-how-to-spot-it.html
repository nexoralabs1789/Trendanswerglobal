How Can You Spot AI-Powered Phishing and Deepfake Scams?

In brief: AI can make fraudulent messages sound polished and impersonation attempts seem persuasive. That does not mean every convincing message is a scam; it means grammar alone is no longer a useful safety test.

What changes when scammers use AI?

Scammers can generate plausible emails, tailor language to a particular recipient and imitate voices or images. CISA warns that such tools can support impersonation and recommends strong account protection, software updates and reporting suspicious messages.

A polished message is not proof of authenticity. Focus on the requested action and verify who is asking.

Six warning signs worth checking

Unexpected urgency: A request to act immediately, especially to avoid a penalty, should prompt independent verification.

Unusual payment instructions: A last-minute bank-account change or gift-card request is a common reason to stop and check.

Credential requests: Treat links asking for passwords, MFA codes or recovery codes with caution.

Lookalike domains: Inspect the full sender address and destination domain rather than the display name alone.

Out-of-channel requests: An unfamiliar number asking you to ignore standard procedures deserves scrutiny.

Suspicious attachments: Do not open unexpected documents or executable files without confirming their origin.

What should you do if a familiar voice asks for money?

Pause and contact the person through a number or channel you already trust. Do not rely on the incoming call, a forwarded contact detail or voice recognition alone. If it involves a workplace payment, follow the usual dual-approval procedure.

What if you already clicked?

Do not enter further information. Change affected passwords from a known-good device, enable multi-factor authentication if it was not already enabled and contact the relevant account provider or IT team. If money is involved, notify the financial institution promptly.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI detectors reliably prove a voice is fake?

Do not rely on a detector as your sole control. Independent identity verification is safer.

Is a message safe when it has perfect grammar?

No. High-quality writing is easy to automate; verify the sender and requested action.
